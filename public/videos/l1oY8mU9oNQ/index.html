Why Quality Striping Matters

Clear parking lines help maximize space utilization, direct traffic efficiently, and support accessibility requirements. Faded or poorly placed markings can create confusion, increase risk of incidents, and negatively affect the property’s professional image.

Techniques and Materials

Line Width and Edge Definition

Professional line striping relies on consistent line widths and high-contrast colors. The use of Sherwin-Williams HotLine traffic paint ensures durability under heavy vehicle traffic and sun exposure.

ADA-Compliant Markings

Markings include accessible parking spaces, access aisles, crosswalks, and appropriate signage to meet current standards and local codes.

Layout and Safety Considerations

An experienced contractor designs layouts that optimize traffic flow, reduce conflicts, and ensure safe pedestrian pathways. Custom markings can support branding while maintaining compliance.

Maintenance and Scheduling

Regular inspection and timely re-striping preserve visibility. A proactive maintenance plan minimizes downtime and ensures continued compliance.
