ADA-Compliant Parking Lot Striping for Clayton Baptist Church

Raleigh Parking Lot Painting LLC delivers durable, compliant pavement markings that enhance accessibility, safety, and the overall visitor experience. In a recent Clayton, North Carolina project, the team refreshed faded lines and added seven new ADA stall spaces, resulting in a clearer, more navigable parking area for parishioners and guests.

Project Overview and Objectives

The transformation focused on restoring legibility, ensuring ADA compliance, and maintaining efficient traffic flow. The full before-and-after reveal demonstrates how careful layout planning and quality materials produce immediate, long-lasting improvements. The team used professional-grade equipment and fast-drying traffic paint to minimize disruption and deliver crisp, durable results.

Techniques and Materials

• High-contrast, durable lines with precise spacing for ADA stalls and accessibility routes.

• Sherwin-Williams HotLine traffic paint known for durable outdoor performance.

• Graco LineLazer-certified tooling ensures consistent line width and straight edges.

• Cleaning and prepping the lot to maximize paint adhesion.

Benefits for Property Owners and Facility Managers

– Improved accessibility and safety for visitors and employees.

– Clear, compliant markings that reduce confusion and traffic conflicts.

– Professionally installed lines that endure weather and wear, reducing maintenance frequency.
